# Idle Cinematics 0.2.0-alpha.1

- Adds ten independently composed camera presets, including hero, profile, overhead, reveal, cave, skyline, golden-hour, and nearby-entity shots.
- Adds context-aware shot selection based on sky visibility, surrounding space, underground location, time of day, and nearby living entities.
- Adds smooth transitions between shots and avoids immediately repeating the same composition.
- Adds an in-game settings screen through the NeoForge Mods menu and the F10 key.
- Adds configurable shot selection, shot duration, camera distance, and nearby-entity framing.
