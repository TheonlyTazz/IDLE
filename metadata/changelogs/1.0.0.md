# Idle Cinematics 1.0.0

First stable release of the clean-room NeoForge implementation.

- Context-aware cinematic shot selection for landscapes, caves, entities, dimensions, and time of day.
- Smooth third-person camera paths with player visibility and terrain visibility support.
- Configurable AFK detection, shot behavior, HUD suppression, and cinematic controls.
- Chat-open AFK activation and nearby entity visibility during cinematic shots.
